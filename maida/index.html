About

Long-term ownership of a SaaS product

I have spent three and a half years on NextAgency, a SaaS portal for insurance agencies, working across its Rails backend and React and Ember front ends. Along the way I helped upgrade it to Rails 6 and Ruby 3, moved its deployment to Docker on AWS ECS, and kept search and the database fast as usage grew. Now I am helping move it from Rails to a Next.js front end and a NestJS back end, building the new pages straight from Figma designs.

I care about clean design patterns, defensive programming and solid fundamentals, and I use AI-assisted development to ship faster. I would like to keep growing on open-source stacks like Ruby on Rails, Ember and Linux at a SaaS product company.
